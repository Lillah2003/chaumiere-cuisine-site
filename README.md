# Chaumière Website

Create a complete, premium, responsive website for the French restaurant LA CHAUMIÈRE, located in Poissy, France.

The website must feel like a real high-quality restaurant website, not a generic template.

RESTAURANT INFORMATION

Name: LA CHAUMIÈRE

Type: French restaurant

Address: 6 Rue au Pain, 78300 Poissy, France

Phone: 01 30 74 44 55

Google rating: 4.4/5 from 594 reviews

Average price: €30–€60 per person

Services:

Dine-in

No takeaway

No delivery

LGBTQ+ friendly

Known opening information:

Lunch service until around 2:00 PM

Evening service from around 7:00 PM

Do NOT invent exact opening days or additional opening hours.

The restaurant recently changed ownership and customer reviews highlight:

warm welcome;

excellent service;

staff who advise customers according to their preferences;

homemade food;

surprising and refined flavours;

pleasant atmosphere;

quality French cuisine.

DESIGN DIRECTION

Create an elegant, warm and refined French restaurant aesthetic.

The design should feel:

authentic;

sophisticated;

intimate;

warm;

contemporary while retaining some traditional French character.

Avoid:

generic restaurant templates;

overly luxurious “Michelin restaurant” styling;

excessive gold;

cliché Parisian imagery;

overly dark interfaces;

excessive animations.

Colour palette

Use a sophisticated natural palette:

warm ivory / cream background;

deep forest green;

dark charcoal;

subtle burgundy accents;

warm beige.

Use lots of whitespace.

Typography

Use an elegant editorial serif font for headings and a clean modern sans-serif font for body text.

Headings should feel similar to a premium French culinary magazine.

Use large typography strategically.

Photography

Photography should be a major part of the experience.

Use large editorial food photography showing refined French dishes, elegant plates, restaurant interiors and table details.

Images should feel natural, warm and authentic rather than like generic stock photography.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE

1. NAVIGATION

Create a clean sticky navigation bar.

Left:
LA CHAUMIÈRE

Navigation:
Accueil
La Carte
Le Restaurant
Avis
Contact

Primary CTA:
Réserver une table

On mobile, create an elegant hamburger menu and keep the reservation action easily accessible.

2. HERO SECTION

Create a large immersive hero section occupying most of the first viewport.

Use a beautiful full-width restaurant or food image.

Add a subtle dark overlay for readability.

Centered content:

Small label:
RESTAURANT FRANÇAIS · POISSY

Large title:
La Chaumière

Subtitle:
Une cuisine française généreuse, faite maison et pleine de saveurs.

Buttons:

Primary:
Réserver une table

Secondary:
Découvrir la carte

At the bottom of the hero, subtly display:

★ 4,4/5 · 594 avis Google

Add a very subtle scroll indicator.

Create an elegant editorial section introducing the restaurant.
